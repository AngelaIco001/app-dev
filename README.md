# My Favorite Series: Harry Potter
 
**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series following the journey of a young wizard attending *Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry* as he uncovers the truth about his past and faces dark forces.
 
## Why I Love It
* **Immersive World-Building:** From Diagon Alley to the Hogwarts castle, the magical setting feels deeply detailed and alive.
* **Memorable Characters:** Watching the trio grow from kids into brave young adults makes the story rewarding.
* **Themes of Friendship:** Loyalty and courage stand at the heart of every installment.
 
### Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. Hermione Granger
2. Harry Potter
3. Draco Malfoy
